> Origin Story

Why I learned to code.

My journey started with digital marketing. While managing SEO, Meta Ads, and analytics, I kept seeing the same problem: businesses were investing heavily in advertising, but their websites were slow, poorly optimized, and missing proper tracking.

No matter how good the campaigns were, performance was limited by technical bottlenecks. Instead of depending on developers to fix those problems, I decided to become one.

I don't build pages because they look impressive. I build pages because they convert visitors into customers.
